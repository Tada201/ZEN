## General Principles

- **Function over Form**: Prioritize utility and performance in all UI/UX developments. Every unique animation, transition, or specialized UI feature must serve a clear functional purpose and be useful for the user's workflow. Avoid performance waste on purely decorative elements.
- **Minimalism**: Maintain a high-density, professional, and minimalist interface. Avoid clutter and redundant visual elements.
- **Performance First**: Ensure that all frontend components are optimized and do not waste CPU/GPU cycles on non-essential visual effects.
